HEALTHCARE & CLINICS

Patient Intake & Booking Engine

THE PROBLEM

Reception phone lines stay busy during clinic hours, leading to patient hold times and missed booking requests.

THE AUTOMATION

Patients message on WhatsApp 24/7 → AI checks doctor schedule → reserves appointment slot → dispatches reminder 2h prior.

THE OUTCOME

Drastically reduced phone interruptions for clinical staff and lower appointment no-show rates.

Customer Support & Status Engine

THE AUTOMATION

AI matches customer phone or order ID with live shipping carrier API and replies with GPS status in seconds.

THE OUTCOME

70%+ of tracking questions resolved instantly without tickets, freeing staff for complex service needs.
