REFERENCE ARCHITECTURES

SYSTEMS WE CAN BUILD FOR YOU.

Here are five concrete examples of automated workflows we deploy for businesses facing response lag, manual data entry, and scheduling overhead.

HIGH-TICKET REAL ESTATE

Lead Qualification & Showing Engine

THE PROBLEM

Inbound buyer inquiries from ads and portals arrive 24/7. Sales teams take 2-4 hours to reply, by which time high-budget buyers have moved on to competitors.

THE AUTOMATION

AI initiates WhatsApp conversation within 15 seconds → asks budget, timeline & layout preferences → matches active inventory → books site walkthrough directly on agent calendar.

THE OUTCOME

100% of leads engaged immediately, zero manual data entry, and sales team spends time only with qualified buyers.

HEALTHCARE & CLINICS

Patient Intake & Appointment Engine

THE PROBLEM

Clinic phone lines are constantly busy, creating patient hold times, missed appointment requests, and high staff phone fatigue.

THE AUTOMATION

Patients message on WhatsApp 24/7 → AI checks doctor schedule → reserves appointment slot in EHR → dispatches automated reminder 2 hours prior to consultation.

THE OUTCOME

Freed reception staff, 24/7 patient booking accessibility, and a noticeable reduction in appointment no-shows.

E-COMMERCE & RETAIL

Post-Purchase & Order Status Engine

THE PROBLEM

Customer service agents spend more than half their workday looking up package tracking numbers across carrier portals.

THE AUTOMATION

AI parses customer order number → queries live carrier API (BlueDart/Delhivery/FedEx) in 80ms → sends instant live tracking link and estimated arrival time.

THE OUTCOME

70%+ of tracking queries solved in seconds with zero staff tickets, leading to higher customer satisfaction.

RESTAURANTS & HOSPITALITY

Table Reservation & Guest Engine

THE PROBLEM

Phone ringing constantly during dinner service distracts host staff, leading to missed calls and lost table covers.

THE AUTOMATION

Guests message on WhatsApp → AI checks floor seating availability → locks reservation → logs dietary preferences in guest CRM for future visits.

THE OUTCOME

Zero hold time for guests, full table capture during rush hours, and enhanced VIP guest hospitality.

B2B PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

Inbound Qualification & Scheduling Pipeline

THE PROBLEM

Corporate inquiries submit web forms, but senior partners take days to review notes and propose meeting times.

THE AUTOMATION

Inbound form enriched with company domain data → AI qualifies case scope and budget → books meeting directly on matching partner's calendar.

THE OUTCOME

Eliminates email tag, locks consultations while client interest is fresh, and keeps CRM deal stages perfectly updated.
